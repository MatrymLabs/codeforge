================================================================================
GREENHOLD - INNER WARDS
================================================================================

Alder Court
[Town Street]
You stand on pale riverstone cobbles between timber houses with painted shutters, where a carved public fountain sends a thin stream into a circular basin.
The air carries lamp oil, wool, and rain, and a smith's measured hammering drifts between the roofs. The town wall rises above the houses to the west.
A lampwright's apprentice waits beside a loaded handcart.
A rack of walking staffs stands near the drainage channel.
Rows of clay jars have been arranged beneath the sheltering eaves.
Obvious exits: east, south, west, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Copper Square
[Market Lane]
Dark brick paving runs through a close district of old guildhalls faced in gray stone, its course marked by a mosaic of the River Verin that winds across the paving.
Children calling across the lane echoes through the district while roasted roots and chimney smoke lingers near the open doorways.
A retired watchman sorts a stack of delivery tags.
A painted notice board stands near the nearest doorway.
Freshly cut flowers have been arranged beneath a striped awning.
Obvious exits: east, south, west, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lantern Walk
[Civic Square]
Wide flagstones of river-worn slate form a quiet civic square lit by hanging iron lanterns that sway gently even in still air. Tall alder trees grow in iron cages at each corner, their leaves whispering against the stone.
The air is cooler here and carries the faint scent of beeswax and old parchment.
A young clerk carries a ledger under one arm.
A pair of street musicians tune a fiddle and a small drum.
Stacks of folded pamphlets rest on a public table.
Obvious exits: north, east, south, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Riverstone Gate
[Town Gate]
Massive double doors of iron-banded oak stand open within a deep arch of pale riverstone. The gatehouse towers rise on either side, their arrow slits dark and watchful.
The smell of wet wool and horse sweat mixes with the cleaner air of the open fields.
A toll chest sits bolted to the gatehouse wall.
A weather-stained map of Veridia is pinned beside the postern door.
Muddy boot prints track in both directions across the threshold.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

================================================================================
ROADS, PATHS AND CONNECTORS
================================================================================

Sunmeadow Road 1
[Trade Road]
A broad packed-earth road runs east-west between low grassy banks. Wildflowers grow thick along the margins and the occasional milestone of pale riverstone marks the distance to Greenhold.
The road smells of dust and warm grass. A light breeze keeps the air moving.
A broken cartwheel leans against a milestone.
A wayside shrine of stacked stones holds a few wilted flowers.
Fresh wagon tracks run in both directions.
Obvious exits: east, west, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sunmeadow Road 2
[Trade Road]
The road continues through more open land. The milestones are regular and the surface is well maintained by local traffic. Meadows stretch away on both sides.
The air is warm and full of the sound of insects and distant birds.
A second wayside shrine stands under a small tree.
Deep ruts show where carts have passed in wetter weather.
A narrow footpath leads off toward a nearby farm.
Obvious exits: east, west, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Elder Path 1
[Forest Edge Road]
The road narrows as it enters the first trees of the eastern woods. Old oaks and beeches rise on either side, their canopy filtering the light into shifting green and gold.
The air cools and grows damp. The smell of leaf mold and wet bark replaces the open meadow scents.
A wooden signpost points east toward Elderwatch and west back to Greenhold.
A large flat stone serves as a natural resting place beside the path.
Fresh deer tracks cross the road and disappear into the undergrowth.
Obvious exits: east, west, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Elder Path 2
[Forest Edge Road]
Deeper into the edge of the wood the trees grow closer and the undergrowth thicker. The road remains clear but the feeling of the forest is stronger. Moss covers many of the stones and roots.
The light is dimmer and greener. Birdsong is constant but the birds themselves are hard to see.
A fallen tree has been cut through to keep the road open.
A small spring rises beside the path and forms a tiny clear pool.
The path continues both deeper into the wood and back toward the open land.
Obvious exits: east, west, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

River Road North 1
[Trade Road]
The well-traveled road follows the western bank of the River Verin. Willows and alders line the water while open pasture stretches inland. Wagon ruts are deep and the road is wide enough for two carts to pass.
The river runs close on the right, its surface bright. The smell of water and wet earth is constant.
A stone milestone marks the remaining distance to Riverbend.
A pair of otters play in the shallows near the bank.
A simple wooden bench has been placed under a large willow for travelers.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

River Road North 2
[Trade Road]
The river road continues northward with the Verin always close on one side. The land on the inland side is a mix of pasture and small cultivated plots.
The air is cool near the water. Occasional fishing platforms jut out over the current.
A second milestone stands beside the road.
A narrow track leads down to a private fishing spot.
The roofs of Riverbend are not yet visible but the traffic has increased.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

River Road South 1
[Trade Road]
Further south the river road climbs a low rise before dropping again toward the southern meadows. The Verin is visible through gaps in the trees and the land opens into broader pasture.
The road surface is firmer here, packed by long use. The air smells of warm dust and distant woodsmoke.
A roadside well stands under a small wooden roof.
A weathered wooden sign points toward Riverbend and Greenhold.
Deep wagon ruts show where carts have struggled in wet weather.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

River Road South 2
[Trade Road]
The southern stretch of the river road runs through more open country. The river is sometimes close, sometimes a short distance away through a belt of trees. Farms appear more frequently.
The air is warmer and the smell of the land is stronger than the river.
A small roadside shrine stands under an oak.
A track leads off toward a visible farmhouse.
The road remains wide and well used.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Riverbend Approach
[Trade Road]
The road drops gently toward the river town of Riverbend. The River Verin glints between the trees to the south. Small farms and orchards begin to appear on either side of the road.
The air carries woodsmoke and the faint sweetness of apple blossom. A dog barks in the distance.
A roadside well stands under a small wooden roof.
A child sits on a fence post watching travelers pass.
Stacks of cut firewood are piled beside the nearest farm gate.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Crossroads Marker
[Crossroads]
Four roads meet at a broad open space marked by a tall riverstone pillar. Carved letters on the pillar give distances to Greenhold, Riverbend, Elderwatch, and the southern meadows.
The wind moves freely here. The air smells of dust and warm grass.
A low stone bench has been built against the base of the pillar.
A small shrine of stacked stones holds a few coins and flowers.
Fresh tracks show recent traffic on all four roads.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Duskwood Boundary
[Forest Edge]
The open meadows end abruptly at the edge of denser woodland. The trees here are older and closer together, their canopy thicker. A rough path continues into the shadows while a clearer track runs along the tree line.
The air cools and the light dims. The smell of leaf mold and damp earth is strong.
A wooden boundary marker stands at the edge of the wood.
A game trail crosses the path and disappears into the undergrowth.
Fallen leaves carpet the ground in thick layers.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Elderwatch Road Marker
[Road]
A tall milestone of pale stone stands where the road to Elderwatch becomes more distinct. The carving is clear and the distances are marked in the local measure.
The surrounding land is a mix of meadow and the first true trees of the eastern wood.
A small lean-to of rough timber offers shelter from rain.
Wagon tracks continue strongly eastward.
The air carries the scent of both open land and forest.
Obvious exits: west, east, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Farm Track North
[Farm Track]
A narrower track leaves the main road and runs toward a cluster of farm buildings visible in the distance. The surface is rougher and the ruts are deeper.
The air smells of the surrounding fields and animals.
A wooden gate stands open across the track.
A scarecrow leans at an angle in a nearby field.
The track continues toward the farm and back to the main road.
Obvious exits: south, north, east, west.

================================================================================
FOREST EDGE, LIGHT FOREST AND STARTER UNDERGROUND
================================================================================

Hill Path 1
[Rolling Hills]
The ground rises into low green hills dotted with outcrops of pale gray stone. Sheep trails wind between the rocks and the wind is stronger here.
The air is clear and carries the scent of thyme and sun-warmed stone. Hawks circle high overhead.
A narrow goat track climbs toward a dark cleft in the nearest outcrop.
A single standing stone leans at a slight angle on the hilltop.
Scattered white bones of a long-dead sheep lie in a hollow.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west, down.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Hill Path 2
[Rolling Hills]
The hills continue with more frequent rocky outcrops and thinner soil. The grass is shorter and mixed with low herbs. The view from the higher points is wide.
The wind is constant. The river and Greenhold are visible to the west on clear days.
A second dark cleft suggests another cave entrance.
A large flat boulder makes a natural lookout point.
Sheep have worn clear paths between the better grazing spots.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west, down.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Moss Cave Mouth
[Cave Entrance]
A low, wide opening in the hillside is half-hidden by hanging moss and ferns. Cool air breathes from the darkness within. The stone around the entrance is smooth and damp.
The smell of wet rock and earth is strong. Water drips steadily somewhere inside.
A small pile of dry wood has been left just inside the entrance by previous travelers.
Scratches on the stone suggest something has used this cave as a den.
A faint green glow is visible deeper in the passage.
Obvious exits: out, down, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Moss Cave
[Shallow Cave]
The passage opens into a low chamber whose floor is soft with centuries of leaf mold and moss. Pale roots hang from the ceiling and a thin stream of water runs along one wall into a clear pool.
The air is cool and still. The only light comes from the entrance and a faint green phosphorescence on the damp walls.
A flat stone near the pool shows signs of old campfires.
A few scattered animal bones lie in a far corner.
The stream continues into a narrower passage leading deeper.
Obvious exits: west, down, out, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lower Moss Passage
[Cave]
The passage narrows and slopes downward. The green glow grows stronger, coming from patches of luminous moss on the walls. The air is colder and the sound of running water is louder ahead.
The floor is uneven and slick in places. Roots still hang from the ceiling but they are thicker and older here.
A small side alcove contains a nest of dried grass and fur.
Water seeps from a crack in the wall and joins the main stream.
The passage continues both downward and slightly to the east.
Obvious exits: up, down, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Glowing Pool Chamber
[Cave]
The passage opens into a rounded chamber dominated by a clear pool that glows with the same soft green light as the moss. The water is cold and still. Stalactites hang from the ceiling and the walls are slick with moisture.
The air is very still and carries only the smell of wet stone and mineral. The glow is strong enough to read by.
A flat ledge of stone runs along one side of the pool.
Small pale fish move slowly in the glowing water.
A narrow crack in the far wall suggests a possible continuation.
Obvious exits: west, up, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Side Alcove
[Cave]
A small dry alcove opens off the main passage. The floor is higher and the air is a little less damp. Someone has used this space as a temporary camp in the past.
The green glow from the main passage provides dim light.
A ring of stones forms an old fire pit.
A few pieces of dry wood and some discarded bones remain.
The alcove offers a place to rest out of the main draft.
Obvious exits: out.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Forest Edge Clearing
[Forest Edge]
A small natural clearing opens among the first true trees of the eastern wood. Sunlight reaches the ground here in shifting patches. The undergrowth is thick around the edges but the center is open and grassy.
The air is cooler and smells strongly of leaf mold and growing things.
A fallen oak provides a natural seat and table.
A faint trail continues deeper into the wood to the east.
Animal tracks of several kinds cross the clearing.
Obvious exits: west, east, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Old Oak Grove
[Light Forest]
Several massive oaks grow close together here, their branches interlocking overhead. The ground is thick with centuries of leaf litter and soft moss. Little undergrowth survives in the deep shade.
The air is still and cool. The only sounds are the occasional creak of old wood and the drip of water from the canopy after rain.
A hollow in the largest oak shows signs of old use as a shelter.
A scatter of acorn shells and small bones lies near the hollow.
Faint paths lead outward between the trunks in several directions.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Deer Trail 1
[Light Forest]
A narrow but clear game trail winds between the trees. The ground is soft and marked with many hoofprints. The canopy is high and the undergrowth is thinner along the trail itself.
The air smells of damp earth and green leaves. Occasional shafts of light reach the forest floor.
A large patch of disturbed earth shows where deer have been bedding.
A broken branch hangs at head height where a large animal passed.
Birds fall silent as travelers move along the trail.
Obvious exits: northeast, southwest, northwest, southeast.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Deer Trail 2
[Light Forest]
The game trail continues deeper into the wood, becoming slightly less distinct in places where the undergrowth closes in. The trees are older and the light is dimmer.
The smell of the forest is strong and complex. Distant bird calls echo among the trunks.
A large fallen tree creates a natural obstacle that the trail detours around.
Moss-covered stones break the surface of the ground in several places.
The trail shows signs of regular use by both animals and the occasional human.
Obvious exits: southwest, northeast, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Beech Hollow
[Light Forest]
A shallow hollow among mature beech trees holds a thicker carpet of leaf litter and a few scattered boulders. The light that reaches the ground is soft and green.
The air is still and quiet. The beech mast underfoot is thick in places.
A large boulder offers a place to sit and rest.
Animal paths cross the hollow in several directions.
The surrounding trees form a natural shelter from wind and light rain.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stream Crossing (Forest)
[Light Forest]
A small clear stream runs through the eastern wood, its banks soft and muddy. Stepping stones have been placed at the best crossing point and the trail continues on the far side.
The sound of running water is pleasant and constant. The air is cooler near the stream.
Moss and ferns grow thickly along the banks.
A fallen log provides an alternative crossing a short distance upstream.
Animal tracks are thick in the soft ground near the water.
Obvious exits: east, west, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Deep Thicket
[Dense Undergrowth]
The undergrowth here is thick with brambles, young trees, and tangled vines. Progress is slow and the trail is faint. The canopy overhead is almost continuous.
The air is still and humid. Visibility is limited to a few paces in any direction.
Thorns catch at clothing and skin with every movement.
A few clearer animal tunnels run through the denser growth.
The sound of the wider forest is muffled by the thickness of the plants.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

================================================================================
ADDITIONAL STARTER UNDERGROUND AND HILLS
================================================================================

Second Cave Mouth
[Cave Entrance]
A narrower opening in a different rocky outcrop leads into another small cave system. The entrance is partially blocked by a fallen stone and the air that issues from it is cool and slightly musty.
Ferns grow thickly around the entrance. The smell of earth and stone is strong.
Scratches on the rock suggest regular use by small animals.
A faint draft indicates the cave continues some distance.
Loose stones make the first steps into the darkness careful work.
Obvious exits: out, down, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Second Cave Chamber
[Cave]
A low, irregular chamber opens beyond the entrance. The floor is uneven rock and the ceiling is low enough that taller travelers must stoop. A few bones and scraps of fur litter one corner.
The air is still and cool. Only faint light reaches this far from the entrance.
A narrow passage continues deeper into the hill.
Water seeps slowly down one wall, leaving a dark stain.
The chamber has the feeling of a place used more by animals than by people.
Obvious exits: out, down, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Root Chamber
[Cave]
Thick tree roots push through the ceiling and walls of this small chamber, creating a tangle of living wood and stone. The air is damp and smells strongly of earth and growing things.
A thin stream of water runs among the roots and disappears into a crack in the floor.
The green glow of luminous moss is present but weaker here.
A small dry ledge offers a place to sit out of the worst of the damp.
The roots form natural handholds and obstacles.
Obvious exits: up, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Overlook Crag
[Hills]
A rocky crag rises above the surrounding hills, offering a wide view across Veridia. The top is mostly bare stone with a few hardy plants growing in cracks. The wind is strong and constant.
From here Greenhold, the river, the meadows, and the edge of the eastern wood are all clearly visible on a clear day.
A natural seat has been worn into the stone by weather and use.
A small cairn of stones stands at the highest point.
The path down is steep but manageable with care.
Obvious exits: down, north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Woolwright's Row
[Craft Lane]
Narrow timber houses lean toward one another over a lane paved with uneven river cobbles. Every doorway displays a hanging sign of spun wool or dyed cloth. Steam rises from open vats behind the houses.
The air is thick with the smell of lanolin, woodsmoke, and wet fleece.
A woman cards wool on a low stool outside her door.
Bales of raw fleece are stacked under a canvas lean-to.
A length of bright green cloth has been stretched on a wooden frame to dry.
Obvious exits: north, south, west, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Temple Court
[Sacred Ground]
Smooth gray flagstones form a quiet courtyard before the Temple of the Verdant. Tall white-barked birches grow in careful rows, their leaves casting shifting green light across the stones.
The air is cooler here and carries the faint scent of crushed mint and old incense.
A young acolyte sweeps the courtyard with a birch-twig broom.
Clay bowls of spring water rest on the altar steps.
A wooden collection box is bolted to the temple wall.
Obvious exits: east, south, west, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Guard Barracks Yard
[Military]
A square of hard-packed earth lies between the town wall and a long stone building with narrow windows. Weapon racks stand under a sloping roof and a pair of practice dummies lean against the wall.
The smell of oil, leather, and damp stone is strong.
A young recruit sits on a bench polishing a helmet.
A water barrel and a dipper stand near the door.
A notice board lists watch rotations in neat handwriting.
Obvious exits: south, west, east, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Baker's Alley
[Side Street]
This narrow alley smells strongly of fresh bread and woodsmoke. Low timber buildings lean close overhead and the cobbles are scattered with flour dust and fallen crumbs.
Warm light spills from an open bakery door.
A flour-dusted apprentice carries a tray of cooling loaves.
A small cat sleeps on a windowsill above a stack of empty flour sacks.
A wooden bench has been placed against the warm bakery wall.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Tannery Lane
[Craft District]
The air here is sharp with the smell of curing hides and woodsmoke. Low stone buildings with wide open doors line a sloping lane that drains toward the river. Stretched hides hang on wooden frames.
A tanner works a hide over a wooden beam.
Bundles of bark and vats of dark liquid stand under a rough shelter.
A large wooden drying rack holds several finished pieces of leather.
Obvious exits: north, south, west, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Harbor Watch Steps
[River Access]
Wide stone steps lead down from the town level toward a small wooden landing on the River Verin. Mooring posts stand in the water and a pair of small boats are tied alongside.
The river smells clean and cold. Gulls call overhead.
A fisherman mends a net on the lowest step.
A coil of rope and a wooden bucket sit near the posts.
A simple sign warns of the current's strength after heavy rain.
Obvious exits: up, north, south, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Civic Hall Entrance
[Civic]
Broad stone steps rise to the heavy oak doors of Greenhold's civic hall. Carved river stones form the lintel and two iron lanterns flank the entrance.
The air smells of old wood and beeswax.
A clerk stands just inside the doorway with a ledger.
A pair of stone benches face each other across the steps.
A bronze plaque records the founding year of the current hall.
Obvious exits: south, east, west, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Old Well Court
[Residential]
A quiet residential court centers on an ancient stone well whose wooden cover is worn smooth by generations of hands. Timber houses with flower boxes surround the space.
The air is still and carries the scent of cooking herbs and damp stone.
A child turns the well handle while an older woman holds a bucket.
Clay pots of herbs grow along the sunny side of the court.
A wooden bench circles part of the well.
Obvious exits: north, east, south, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Smithy Lane
[Craft Street]
The ringing of hammer on anvil is constant here. Open-fronted forges line both sides of a short, soot-stained street. Sparks fly into the air with every strike and the heat is noticeable even from the middle of the lane.
The air smells of hot metal, coal smoke, and quenched iron.
A burly smith works a glowing billet on a large anvil.
Tongs, hammers, and unfinished tools hang from the forge walls.
A water trough for quenching stands outside the nearest forge.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Herbalist's Row
[Quiet Street]
Narrow timber houses with green-painted shutters line this quieter street. Bundles of drying herbs hang from almost every eave and the air is thick with the scent of mint, yarrow, and dried lavender.
A small garden of medicinal plants grows in a fenced plot beside one house.
An older woman sorts dried leaves on a long wooden table.
Clay jars and small cloth bags are arranged neatly on a shelf under the eaves.
A wooden sign shaped like a mortar and pestle hangs above the nearest door.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Candlewright Street
[Craft Street]
The smell of beeswax and tallow is strong along this short street of candle makers. Windows display rows of finished candles in many sizes and the occasional colored taper.
Warm yellow light spills from several open workshops even in daylight.
A young apprentice dips wicks into a pot of melted wax.
Racks of cooling candles stand in neat rows under a lean-to.
A large iron cauldron of wax simmers over a low fire.
Obvious exits: north, south, west, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

River View Terrace
[Overlook]
A short row of houses stands on a slight rise with clear views down to the River Verin. The street is paved with the same pale riverstone as the rest of the inner wards and a low stone wall runs along the river side.
The air is fresher here and carries the scent of the water below.
A woman sits on the wall sewing while watching the river.
Clay flower pots line several windowsills.
A narrow set of steps leads down toward the harbor landing.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, down.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Market Lane East
[Market]
This continuation of the market district is narrower and more crowded with permanent stalls. Cloth awnings create a patchwork of shade and the ground is littered with discarded vegetable leaves and straw.
The mixed smells of produce, cheese, and fresh bread fill the air.
A cheese seller cuts samples from a large wheel.
Baskets of early apples and root vegetables sit on low tables.
A small boy tries to interest passersby in wooden whistles.
Obvious exits: west, east, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Quiet Court
[Residential]
A small, almost hidden court opens off the main streets. The cobbles are cleaner here and a single young tree grows in the center. The surrounding houses are modest but well-kept.
The air is still and quiet. Only distant town sounds reach this place.
A wooden bench sits beneath the tree.
A pair of laundry lines stretch between upper windows.
A cat watches from a sunny windowsill.
Obvious exits: west, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wallside Alley
[Side Alley]
This narrow alley runs hard against the inner face of the town wall. The stones of the wall are cool and damp and the alley itself is always in shadow. Drainage channels run along the base of the wall.
The air smells of wet stone and moss.
A stack of firewood has been piled against the wall.
A narrow wooden door set into the wall leads to a postern.
Moss and small ferns grow in the cracks between the wall stones.
Obvious exits: north, south, east.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fountain Square
[Civic]
A larger public fountain dominates this open square. Water spills from the mouths of carved river fish into a wide circular basin. Benches of pale stone surround the fountain and the square is popular at most hours.
The sound of falling water is constant and pleasant. The air is cooler near the basin.
Children sail small wooden boats in the fountain.
A street vendor sells roasted nuts from a small cart.
Pigeons strut around the basin edge looking for scraps.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

================================================================================
GREENHOLD - OUTER WARDS AND GATES
================================================================================

North Market
[Outer Market]
Open-air stalls of rough timber and canvas fill a broad packed-earth square just inside the northern wall. Merchants call their wares while carts rattle in from the river road.
The mixed smells of fresh bread, smoked fish, and horse dung hang in the air.
A spice merchant arranges clay jars on a low table.
A boy sells roasted chestnuts from a handcart.
A pair of dogs sleep beneath an empty stall.
Obvious exits: south, east, west, north.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

South Pasture Gate
[Town Edge]
A simple wooden gate stands open in the low stone wall that marks the southern edge of Greenhold. Beyond it the land falls away into rolling green pasture dotted with sheep.
The wind carries the clean smell of grass and damp earth.
A shepherd leans on the gatepost chewing a stem of grass.
A half-empty water trough stands just inside the wall.
Fresh hoofprints and sheep droppings mark the track.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

East Wall Path
[Town Wall]
A narrow path of packed earth runs along the inside of Greenhold's eastern wall. Arrow slits open at regular intervals and the wall walk can be reached by wooden ladders.
The wind is stronger here and carries the scent of the open fields beyond the wall.
A ladder leans against the wall leading up to the walk.
A stack of spare spears rests under a small shelter.
Moss grows thick on the lower stones of the wall.
Obvious exits: north, south, west, up.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

West Wall Path
[Town Wall]
This stretch of wall path runs along the western side of Greenhold where the land drops more sharply toward the river. The stones are darker with age and damp.
The river can be heard faintly below. The air smells of wet stone and willow.
A guard leans on the parapet looking westward.
A small iron-bound chest is bolted near the postern.
Ivy has begun to climb the lower courses of the wall.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, down.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stable Yard
[Outer Ward]
A wide yard of hard earth and scattered straw stands beside a long timber stable. Horses shift in their stalls and the smell of hay, manure, and leather is strong.
A stablehand brushes down a bay mare.
Bales of hay are stacked against the stable wall.
A wooden mounting block stands near the yard entrance.
Obvious exits: north, east, south, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Carters' Rest
[Outer Yard]
An open yard just inside the southern gate serves as a resting place for wagons and carters. Several carts stand with their shafts raised and horses crop grass along the wall.
The air smells of dust, horse, and woodsmoke from a small cookfire.
A carter sits on a wagon bed eating bread and cheese.
A water barrel and dipper stand under a lean-to.
Spare wagon wheels lean against the wall in a neat row.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

North Gate Approach
[Outer Gate]
The northern gate of Greenhold is smaller than the main riverstone gate but still solid. Two guard towers flank a heavy wooden door that stands open during daylight hours. The road beyond leads into the northern pastures.
The air is cooler and carries the scent of open grassland.
A pair of guards check the occasional cart.
A wooden board lists current tolls and notices.
Mud from the road has been tracked across the threshold.
Obvious exits: south, north, east, west.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

East Gate Path
[Outer Gate]
A secondary gate opens eastward toward the meadows and the road to Elderwatch. The gate is simpler, with a single tower and a reinforced wooden door. Outside, the land opens quickly into pasture.
The wind carries the smell of grass and distant forest.
A guard sits on a stool just inside the gate.
A small shrine to safe travel stands beside the gatepost.
Wagon ruts deepen immediately beyond the threshold.
Obvious exits: west, east, north, south.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wall Walk North
[Town Wall]
The wall walk here offers a clear view northward across the pastures. The stone parapet is chest-high and worn smooth by years of weather and watchmen. Ladders lead down to the outer wards at intervals.
The wind is strong and clean. Greenhold's rooftops spread out to the south.
A guard paces slowly along the walk.
A covered signal fire pit sits ready but unlit.
Arrow slits look outward across the open land.
Obvious exits: south, east, west, down.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wall Walk South
[Town Wall]
