Caeloria High Square
[City Square]
Broad flagstones of pale gray stone form the central square of Caeloria City. A tall statue of a crowned figure stands at the center, its base carved with the names of past rulers. Tall buildings of stone and timber surround the open space.
The air carries the mixed scents of the city — cooking, horses, incense, and distant sea. Voices and the clatter of carts fill the square.
A pair of city guards stand near the statue.
A public notice board is fixed to the base of the monument.
Street musicians play near the northern edge of the square.
Obvious exits: north, south, east, west.

[City Street]

The air is cleaner here than in the lower markets. The sound of the city is present but less chaotic.

Flower boxes brighten many of the upper windows.
