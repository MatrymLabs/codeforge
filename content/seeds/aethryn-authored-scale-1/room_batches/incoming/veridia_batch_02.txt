Alder Court
[Town Street]

You stand on pale riverstone cobbles between timber houses with painted shutters, where a carved public fountain sends a thin stream into a circular basin.

The air carries lamp oil, wool, and rain, and a smith's measured hammering drifts between the roofs. The town wall rises above the houses to the west.

A lampwright's apprentice waits beside a loaded handcart.
A rack of walking staffs stands near the drainage channel.
Rows of clay jars have been arranged beneath the sheltering eaves.

Obvious exits: east, south.

[Market Lane]
