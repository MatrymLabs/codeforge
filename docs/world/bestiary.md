# Bestiary — the Living (and Unliving) Things of Aethryn

*Every creature belongs to an ecosystem; every ecosystem has a food chain; every creature has a
reason to be where it is. Aethryn's life divides into three great orders by its relationship to Ember,
and each Reach grows its own web of predator, prey, and salvager on top of that division. This is the
framework and the first roster; the world grows hundreds of creatures against this pattern.*

Each entry carries: **habitat · behavior · intelligence · social organization · combat style ·
weakness · strength · loot & crafting materials · ecological role · rare/boss/legendary variants.**
Combat maps to the engine's elemental system — a creature's blows carry an **element** (its nature),
and its **resistances** name what it shrugs off and what it fears (see the flagship seed). *Read a
creature before you fight it; the world rewards the player who learns the web.*

---

## The Three Orders of Aethryn's Life

1. **The Grown** — ordinary life, precipitated out of wild Ember long ago and now breeding true:
   beasts, birds, fish, the honest fauna of a living world. They have habitats, mates, and fear. Most
   of the Verdance and the green Reaches. *Killable, edible, huntable; the base of every food chain.*
2. **The Forged** — things a maker *made* and that outlived their maker: Forgework beasts, guardian
   constructs, the Forgeborn of the Kollforge. They do not breed; they *persist*, running an ancient
   purpose no one remembers. *Elemental, tireless, and often guarding something.*
3. **The Unforged** — entropy made hostile: wights, wraiths, and the half-real horrors of the
   Cinderdeep and the Unforging scars, made of what unmaking left behind. They are not dead things;
   they are *unmade* things, and they hunger to unmake more. *The setting's true monsters.*

A boss is usually the apex of its web; a **legendary variant** is a named individual with a history
the world remembers — a hunt worth an expansion.

## Ecosystem: The Verdance (the living wild) — *the Grown, at their richest*

- **Thornback boar** *(Grown, prey-turned-dangerous)* — Habitat: understory ember-loam. Behavior:
  roots, charges when cornered. Combat: earth-charge (ERT). Weakness: outmaneuvered. Loot: hide,
  tusk-ember. Role: keystone forager; where the boars thin, the canopy overgrows.
- **Canopy-stalker** *(Grown, apex)* — Habitat: mountain-trees. Behavior: ambush from above, patient,
  territorial. Combat: drop-strike, high evasion. Weakness: forced to ground. Loot: silent-hide (a
  stealth crafting material). Role: apex canopy predator; culls the stalk-kin below it. Variant: the
  **Boughwarden**, a legendary stalker the Deeprooted half-worship.
- **Mire-returned** *(Unforged, in a living land)* — Habitat: breathing swamps where Cinder seeps up.
  Behavior: the swamp's dead, half-re-risen, drawn to warmth. Combat: venom-touch (PSN). Weakness:
  fire, radiance. Role: the Verdance's reminder that even the most alive Reach has a mouth into the
  deep. Boss: the **Heart-Grove**, a whole dungeon-organism whose "creatures" are its immune system.

## Ecosystem: The Rimefall (the quenched north) — *the Forged and Unforged, preserved in ice*

- **Glass-hound** *(Forged, precipitated from frozen wild Ember)* — Habitat: the ice-fields. Behavior:
  packs, silent, drawn to sound. Combat: frost-bite (ICE). Weakness: fire, sudden heat. Loot:
  glass-ember (a rare cold-craft material). Role: the ice's apex pack-hunter.
- **Rime-wight** *(Unforged, flash-frozen)* — Habitat: the Frozen Court and the ice-cities. Behavior:
  the dead of the Unforging, paused mid-task, waking if disturbed. Combat: chill-grasp (ICE), resists
  frost, weak to flame. Weakness: fire. Role: the Rimefall's warning made flesh — *touch nothing.*
  Boss: the **Frozen Court King**, an Emberwright preserved entire, whose waking would restart an
  Unforging mid-freeze.

## Ecosystem: The Cinderwaste (the grey Reach) — *the Unforged, under an open sky*

- **Ash-jackal** *(Grown, adapted to ruin)* — Habitat: the salt-road's edge. Behavior: packs, scavenge
  and hunt. Combat: bite. Weakness: none special; a honest early threat. Loot: ash-hide. Role: the
  waste's scavenger-predator, the base of what little food chain the grey supports.
- **Salvage-wraith** *(Unforged, made of leavings)* — Habitat: the Scoured Ruins. Behavior: drifts the
  ruin, made of what the Unforging left, drawn to the living. Combat: unmaking-touch (DRK). Weakness:
  radiance, being *finished* (a Forger can sometimes re-forge one to peace). Role: the ruin's
  guardian-by-accident. Boss: the **Cinder-Colossus**, a welded giant the waste grew around a scar
  (ERT, resists stone, weak to gale).

## Ecosystem: The Quenchmere (the drowned reach) — *the Grown of the water and the Unforged of the wreck*

- **Tide-crawler** *(Grown, littoral)* — Habitat: the wrack line. Behavior: territorial, clacking.
  Combat: brine-claw (WTR). Weakness: high ground. Loot: brine-ember, shell-plate. Role: the shallows'
  scavenger; the fisherfolk's daily nuisance and catch.
- **Drowned pilot** *(Unforged, of the wreck)* — Habitat: the reefs of sunken ships. Behavior: the dead
  who still keep the wheel, holding a course to nowhere. Combat: flood-grasp (WTR), resists flood,
  **weak to lightning** (water conducts). Weakness: LGT. Role: the wreck-lane's guardian. Boss: the
  **Leviathan of the Deep Lanes**, a Forged beast the Emberwrights made to keep the seas and never
  called home.

## Ecosystem: The Kollforge (the molten reach) — *the Forged, still hot*

- **Cinder-drake** *(Forged, of the vents)* — Habitat: lava-shelves. Behavior: territorial, drawn to
  the hottest Ember. Combat: flame-breath (FIR). Weakness: frost, water. Loot: drake-scale (a heat-craft
  material). Role: apex of the molten shelf.
- **Forgeborn** *(Forged, never cooled)* — Habitat: the Anvil-Vents. Behavior: living Forgework running
  an ancient purpose. Combat: forge-strike (FIR), resists flame. Weakness: ICE. Role: the vent's
  guardian, guarding raw Ember the boldest Forgers covet. Boss: the **Vent-Lord**, a Forgeborn grown
  vast on a millennium of raw fire.

## Ecosystem: The Sundered Sky (the floating reach) — *the Grown of the high air, the Unforged of the fall*

- **Stormkin** *(Grown, aerial)* — Habitat: the wind-currents between shards. Behavior: flocks, ride
  the storms. Combat: gale-strike (WND/LGT). Weakness: still air. Loot: storm-quill. Role: the sky's
  apex flyer.
- **Fall-wight** *(Unforged, of the tearing)* — Habitat: the raw edges of the loose lands. Behavior:
  those the Unforging took when the land came loose, still falling in a way that never ends. Combat:
  vertigo-touch (DRK/WND). Weakness: radiance, solid ground. Role: the sky's horror. Boss: the
  **Drifting Court**, a wandering Emberwright shard-city that is never in the same place twice.

## The Vertical Planes

- **The Great Spiral** — themed **Gate-wardens** by Coil (fire, frost, storm, shadow, stone), each a
  Forged guardian that resists its own element and fears a counter; the climb is an elemental gauntlet
  toward the **Spiral Sovereign** at the summit. (Built by the flagship seed's procedural Spiral.)
- **The Cinderdeep** — the **Unforged** at their purest, deepening without floor: drowned makers,
  hollow smiths, the things the world dropped. The deeper you sink, the less alive and the more hostile
  the web, until the creatures are barely creatures at all — just unmaking, hungry.

## The pattern for growth

Every new creature must answer: *what does it eat, what eats it, why is it here, what does the world
lose if it dies, and what does its body give a crafter.* A Reach is not populated by dropping monsters
into rooms; it is grown as a **web** — one apex, its prey, its scavengers, its Forged guardians, and
the Unforged that seep in from the deep — so that a player who hunts one thread feels the whole
ecosystem answer. Boss and legendary variants are the *named* nodes of that web, with histories the
world's songs and journals remember. This is how hundreds of creatures stay meaningful instead of
becoming a stat-list: each one belongs.
