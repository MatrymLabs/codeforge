# Continental Atlas — the Reaches of the Anvil

*The Anvil is the world's forged surface. Before the Unforging it was one joined supercontinent; the
Sundering cracked it along the cooling-seas into seven **Reaches**, tore some lands loose into the
sky, and dropped others into the Cinderdeep. Each Reach is designed to support its own RPG: a distinct
biome, culture, government, threat, and level band, with its own capitals, wilds, ruins, and secrets.
The **Forgeward Road** runs east from the surface toward the Forge, and the **Cinderdeep** falls
below, so the map grows outward and down forever.*

Consistency note: **Emberreach** is the starting Reach, and the flagship `aethryn` seed builds its
Kindlands coast and edges. Emberreach carries a *taste* of every biome (a local Ashwaste, a
Cooling-Sea shore, the Reachwood, a Cinderdeep mouth); the *full* continental expression of each
biome is a sibling Reach across the seas — a whole expansion continent. This keeps the starting land
complete while leaving the world vast.

> **How to read a Reach.** Each entry answers the content-density questions: *identity, level band,
> geography, people & government, landmark settlements, factions present, signature dangers &
> dungeons, and the hooks that draw a player across the sea to reach it.*

## I. Emberreach - the Cradle (the starting Reach) · levels 1-30 + the Forgeward Road east

**Identity.** Temperate river-valleys where the first Gate reopened and the Rekindling was dated. The
"everyman" continent: optimistic, green, walkable, the place a Forger is born. It is also the most
*relit* land — its capitals have working forges again — which makes it the hub the other Reaches
trade toward and the Wardens watch closest.

**Geography.** The **Kindlands** (starting valleys, the tutorial coast), the **Wardenmarch** (fortified
highlands where the Wardens hold the old Gates), a local **Ashwaste** and a **Cooling-Sea** shore
(the Sundermere's edge), the **Reachwood** (a wild-Ember forest), and the **Coilfoot Range** where the
Forgeward Road begins its run east. The **Ember-roads** thread it capital to capital.

**People & government.** The **Reachfolk** (open, hopeful, "anyone can learn to Forge") under a
patchwork of **Kindled Crowns** and city-republics of the **Free Forges**, with the **Warden Concord**
holding the marches. All three great powers meet here, which is why Emberreach is where their argument
is loudest and its quests densest.

**Landmark settlements.** **Cinderhearth** (the coast town, first relit forge); **Emberreach City**
(the first capital — the Grand Forge, Orders' Row, the Market Quarter, the Warden Gate); **Quench
Harbor** (the Cooling-Sea port); the **Ashborn Camp** (survivors in the local waste); frontier holds
along the Wardenmarch.

**Factions present.** All of them, in miniature — the Concord (marches), the Free Forges (city halls),
the Kindled Crowns (the capital), the Emberwright Rememberers (the Grand Archive), the Salvage Guilds
(the harbor and the waste).

**Dangers & dungeons.** Reach wolves and thornback boars in the wilds; the **Cold Cellar** and the
**Cinderdeep mouth** below; the **Cinderheart** at the bottom of the local deep; the **Bramblewarren**;
the **Wreck Reef**; the **Glass Crater** of the local Ashwaste. The tutorial threat is gentle
(training grounds catch a fall); the deep and the Forgeward Road are not.

**Hooks.** Wake with no memory, relight the coast, choose a road (east on the Forgeward Road, down the
deep, out to the wilds or the sea), and follow the scattered craft toward the capital and the world beyond.

## II. The Rimefall — the Quenched North · levels 30–50

**Identity.** A continent **flash-quenched** in the Unforging: caught mid-Forge and frozen in an
instant, so its Emberwright cities still stand *complete* under the ice, mid-gesture, better-made than
anything alive. The most beautiful and the most haunted Reach — a museum of the golden age, kept by
cold and by the things the cold preserved.

**Geography.** Glacier-fields over drowned valleys; the **Riming Sea** of grinding floes; frozen
Forgeworks that ring like struck iron; the **Stillsong** ice-shelf where sound carries for miles and
nothing should be said aloud.

**People & government.** The **Rimekin** — few, austere, oath-bound to *touch nothing*, because in the
Rimefall the past is not ruined but *paused*, and to disturb it is to risk restarting an Unforging
mid-freeze. A quiet theocracy of the **Silent Anvil**, who hold this whole Reach as a shrine to
restraint.

**Landmark settlements.** **Stillhearth** (a monastery-port on the ice-edge); the **Frozen Court** (an
Emberwright capital preserved entire, walked but never touched); scattered warm-camps of Ashborn
salvagers who defy the Rimekin to loot the ice.

**Dangers & dungeons.** **Rime-wights** (the flash-frozen dead, still mid-task); **glass-hounds**
(precipitated out of frozen wild Ember); the **Frozen Court** itself as a dungeon where every room is
a perfect, lethal tableau; the **Stillsong** where speaking wakes what sleeps.

**Hooks.** The Rimefall is where a Forger can *see* the golden age instead of imagining it — and where
the Silent Anvil's terrible question (*should the craft be relearned at all?*) is not a debate but a
frozen landscape you are walking through.

## III. The Verdance — the Living Wild · levels 20–45

**Identity.** Where wild Ember was never gathered or shaped and simply **grew** — a primeval
jungle-swamp continent that is, in the most literal sense, alive: its rivers choose their courses,
its forests remember trespass, its weather has moods. The world at its most generative and least
governable, and the strongest case that the craft should be *negotiated with*, not commanded.

**Geography.** Canopy-cities in trees the size of mountains; **breathing swamps** where Cinder seeps up
and the dead half-return; ember-blooms that light the understory; rivers that run uphill where the
Forgework beneath still holds a slope that gravity forgot.

**People & government.** The **Deeprooted** — animist, decentralized, treating wild Ember as sacred and
Forging as a *conversation* with a living world rather than an imposition on a dead one. No crown; a
web of **grove-councils** and reader-elders who broker between the people and the wilds.

**Landmark settlements.** **Highbough** (the canopy capital, a city grown rather than built);
**Mirefast** (a swamp-edge trade-stilt town); hidden groves that move.

**Dangers & dungeons.** Believable, layered **ecosystems** with real food chains (see the
[Bestiary](bestiary.md)): apex canopy-hunters, swarm-kin, the swamp's returning dead; the **Heart-Grove**
(a dungeon that is a single vast living organism); poacher-ruins where someone tried to *harvest* the
Verdance and the Verdance ate them.

**Hooks.** The Verdance is the exploration-and-ecology Reach: reward curiosity, read the wild, and
learn that the First Seed's dream — a self-Forging world — is not a lost ideal here but a *dangerous
present*, growing without a maker's hand, exactly as the Emberwrights intended and no one can control.

## IV. The Cinderwaste — the Grey Reach · levels 15–40

**Identity.** A continent **half-Unforged**: a land the Sundering did not drop into the Cinderdeep but
did not spare either, scoured to grey Cinder and glass. Salvage, danger, and the richest rare Ember in
the world, because a place this close to unmaking is also close to raw possibility. The surface's
answer to the Cinderdeep — an underworld you can walk under an open, bleached sky.

**Geography.** Dunes of cold ash; **glass craters** where the Unforging struck; half-standing
Forge-holds scoured to skeletons; salt-flats over drowned canals; the **Ashline**, a horizon-long scar
where the world simply *stops being finished*.

**People & government.** The **Ashborn** — survivors of an Unforging, fatalistic and resourceful, who
do not mourn the world and do not make it; they *salvage* it, selling the past back to itself piece by
piece. No government to speak of; salvage-clans and a hard code of the road.

**Landmark settlements.** **Saltring** (a salvage-city ringing a dry sea); wandering **Ashborn camps**;
the **Glass Market**, a black-market bazaar built in a crater where anything Unforged can be bought.

**Dangers & dungeons.** **Ash-jackals** and **salvage-wraiths** (made of what the Unforging left);
**cinder-colossi** (welded giants the waste grew around a scar); the **Scoured Ruins** (half-Forged
dungeons where the walls end in mid-air); pockets of *live* Unforging that can still come apart at a
touch — the deadliest terrain in the surface world.

**Hooks.** The Cinderwaste is the salvage-and-risk Reach, and the one that whispers the Cinder
Mysteries' heresy: *unmaking is holy too, because without Cinder there is no new Ember.* Rare Ember and
Unforged relics draw every faction here, and every faction's ethics fray in the grey.

## V. The Quenchmere — the Drowned Reach · levels 10–40

**Identity.** The Reach the **cooling-seas** rose over — a vast archipelago of ports, drowned
Forge-cities, and reefs of sunken ships, first-quenched and never fully drained. Cosmopolitan,
mercantile, lawless at the edges: the world's trade-lung and its pirate-coast, where the drowned past
is a fishery and a graveyard at once.

**Geography.** Ten thousand islands over sunken capitals; the **Wreckways** (reef-lanes marked by the
masts of drowned ships); tidal ruins that surface at low water; the **Quench Deeps** where whole
Forge-cities lie lit and cold below the keels.

**People & government.** The **Merewrights** — seafaring salvagers and traders, cosmopolitan and
sharp; a loose **Port-Accord** of harbor-republics held together by trade and threatened by the
**Salvage Guilds** who want the drowned cities opened and the pirate-clans who want the lanes free.

**Landmark settlements.** **Tidewharf** (the great free-port capital, built across a hundred hulls);
**Quench Harbor** (Emberreach's shore, the near edge); drowned **Sunhold** (a capital under the keels,
walked in diving-bells and drowned-lung Forgework).

**Dangers & dungeons.** **Tide-crawlers**, **drowned pilots** (the dead who still keep the wheel),
brine-wights, and the leviathan-kin of the deep lanes; the **Drowned Sunhold** (a submerged capital
dungeon, lit and cold); pirate-holds in surfaced ruins.

**Hooks.** The Quenchmere is the trade-and-exploration Reach and the world's crossroads — every other
Reach is reached across its water. It is also where the Sundermere's drowned Forge-cities hold the
largest surviving *unbroken* Forgework outside the Rimefall, which makes the Deeps the richest and
most contested salvage in the world.

## VI. The Kollforge — the Molten Reach · levels 40–60

**Identity.** The surface land closest to the **Forge's** heat: a volcanic continent where the world is
still visibly *being made*, its Forgework hot and active, its ground remade by every eruption. Home of
the boldest and most dangerous Forgers, who work directly in living fire and count a cooled land as a
dead one. The high-level surface Reach and the last stop before the overland endgame.

**Geography.** Rivers of Forgelight-lit lava; the **Anvil-Vents** where raw Ember still wells up from
the Forge below; obsidian cities on cooling shelves; ash-storms that reshape the map between visits.

**People & government.** The **Kollkin** — fire-Forgers, proud and fatal, organized into
**Vent-Forges** (part guild, part cult) that compete to work the hottest, rawest Ember. The Free
Forges' most radical wing thrives here; the Wardens are barely tolerated.

**Landmark settlements.** **Emberkoll** (the molten capital, an obsidian city over a lightning-lit
caldera); **Venthold** (a Vent-Forge fortress-guild); pilgrim-camps of Forgers come to work the raw.

**Dangers & dungeons.** **Cinder-drakes**, **magma-kin**, and the **Forgeborn** (living Forgework that
never cooled); the **Anvil-Vents** as a dungeon-frontier where the deeper you go the closer to raw
creation and unmaking at once; ruins of Forgers who reached too hot.

**Hooks.** The Kollforge is where a Forger stops *learning* the craft and starts *risking* it at the
source's edge: the on-ramp to the Forgeward Road and the Forge itself, and the Reach that makes the
endgame's temptation (to make a world) feel not theoretical but *close*.

---

## VII. The Sundered Sky — the Floating Reach · levels 50–70

**Identity.** The lands the Unforging **tore loose** that never fell: shards of the old
supercontinent hanging in the high air on stubborn Forgework, drifting slowly on the world's winds.
The threshold Reach: where the surface Reaches give onto the Forgeward Road. Awe-inspiring, lonely,
and the last shard of the surface world before the Road runs east to the Forge.

**Geography.** Floating isles trailing waterfalls into cloud; **sky-bridges** of surviving Ember-road
hung between shards; wind-currents that are the only roads; the **Coilfoot** anchor-line running from
the greatest shard down to the marches of the Forgeward Road.

**People & government.** The **Skyborn** - few, adapted, half-Warden and half-hermit, who keep the
sky-Gates and the way onto the Forgeward Road. The Warden Concord's most important and most isolated
holding: the last threshold before the endgame, guarded hardest of all.

**Landmark settlements.** **Highgate** (the sky-Warden capital at the Coilfoot, the door to the
Forgeward Road); drifting **anchor-holds** on the larger shards; the wreck-camps of Forgers who tried
to fly and did not.

**Dangers & dungeons.** **Sky-leviathans**, **stormkin**, and **fall-wights** (those the loose lands
took); the **Drifting Court** (a wandering Emberwright shard-city, a dungeon that is never in the same
place twice); the long, lethal traverse of the Coilfoot anchor-line itself.

**Hooks.** The Sundered Sky is the last surface Reach and the door to the endgame: reach Highgate,
pass the sky-Warden's test, and set out east on the Forgeward Road toward the Forge and the answer
to why the first makers stopped.

## The Frontier and the Deep

### The Forgeward Road - the Overland Endgame · levels 43-300
The long run to the world's far heart: **marches** running east from the Coilfoot toward the **Forge**,
each march a band of the world nearer the source, each **waystation** a threshold kept by a
**road-warden**. The overland endgame and the setting's true destination: reaching the Forge's Edge,
becoming a maker of worlds, and confronting the choice about the First Seed. (The flagship seed builds
the first marches and the procedural run east to the **Sovereign** at the Forge's Edge, the level-300
cap; see the [Build Order](build_order.md).) The further east you go, the softer the world's laws and
the closer the Kindling's raw dawn.

### The Cinderdeep — the Descending Frontier · levels 10 → the deepening cap
What the Unforging **unmade**: caverns, drowned cities, and half-real regions where shapes never
re-cooled, home of the **Unforged** (entropy made hostile). The dungeon-continent that grows *downward*
forever — every Reach has a mouth into it, and the deeper you sink the more the world comes apart. The
question the Cinderdeep asks is the mirror of the Road's: *is the deep the world's grave, or its
compost — can the Unforged be rekindled, or only contained?*

---

## The shape of the whole world

Horizontal (the seven Reaches across the seas, and the Forgeward Road running east to the Forge),
vertical-down (the Cinderdeep). A Forger begins on the green cradle of Emberreach, crosses the
Quenchmere's water to the frozen north, the living wild, the grey waste, and the molten edge, passes
through the Sundered Sky onto the Forgeward Road, and sinks through every Reach's mouth into the
deepening dark, and at the far end of both axes waits the same thing: the Forge, the First Seed, and
the world as it was before it broke. The map does not end. That is the point.
